When replacement makes sense

Why Homeowners Replace an Older Furnace

If your current furnace is unreliable, inefficient, or nearing the end of its service life, replacement can often be the better long-term choice. A newer system can improve comfort, dependability, and heating performance.

Frequent Repairs

If your furnace needs repeated service, repair costs can add up quickly.

Uneven Heating

Older systems can struggle to heat the home evenly and may cause hot and cold spots.

Higher Energy Use

Low-efficiency or aging furnaces can cost more to operate during the heating season.

Noisy Operation

Loud startup, blower noise, or rattling can be a sign your system is wearing out.

Poor Reliability

An older furnace may become less dependable when you need it most.

Comfort & Safety

A professionally installed new furnace can improve comfort and provide more confidence in everyday operation.

Every home is different, but a typical furnace replacement includes the core work needed for a clean and professional installation.

  • Installation of the new furnace

Common questions

Furnace Installation FAQ

How much does furnace replacement cost?

Typical replacement ranges vary by equipment type, efficiency, and installation complexity. Standard projects are often $3,500–$7,000, while premium or high-efficiency projects are often $4,500–$9,000. A written estimate is provided after an in-home assessment.

How long does furnace installation take?

Many standard furnace replacements can be completed in one day, but timing depends on the equipment and the work required.

Should I repair or replace my furnace?

That depends on the age of the system, the repair needed, and the overall condition of the equipment. If repair costs are high or the furnace is near the end of its service life, replacement may be the better long-term option.

What affects the final furnace price?

System size, efficiency, venting, thermostat needs, duct transitions, access, and overall installation complexity all affect the final price.

Can I keep my existing thermostat?

In many cases yes, but sometimes a thermostat upgrade is recommended for better compatibility or performance.

Do I need ductwork changes?

Not always. Some projects only need a straightforward replacement, while others benefit from small duct or transition adjustments.

Can I finance a new furnace?

Yes. Financing options are available for qualifying customers.

Do you provide an in-home estimate?

Yes. We provide in-home assessments so we can recommend appropriate equipment and give you clear pricing.
