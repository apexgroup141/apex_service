Repair or replace?

When a Repair Makes Sense

Most service calls end with a repair: a failed capacitor, a clogged drain, a dirty coil or a bad sensor. When the system is in good shape, fixing the failed part is the right call.

Sometimes the diagnostic shows a bigger problem: an old system with a failed compressor, repeated refrigerant leaks or repairs that keep coming back. In that case we tell you honestly and can price a replacement too, so you can compare both options. The choice is always yours.

Regular maintenance helps avoid many of these calls. Mini-splits in particular collect dust and buildup in the blower wheel and coil, which reduces airflow and can cause odors and leaks. A deep cleaning brings the unit back to how it should run. Read more: when HVAC repair makes sense

Between visits, our HVAC maintenance checklist covers what you can check yourself, and if your heat pump is blowing cold air, start with these safe checks.

Common questions

HVAC Repair & Maintenance FAQ

How much does a diagnostic visit cost?

The diagnostic visit is $50. If you approve the repair and we do it, the $50 is credited toward the repair price.

Can you come the same day?

We come the same day whenever the schedule allows, and we accept emergency calls. We work Monday through Saturday and are closed on Sunday.

Which brands do you service?

All brands, including Mitsubishi, Daikin, Fujitsu, LG, Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Goodman, York and others.

How much does mini-split cleaning cost?

$250 for the outdoor unit plus one indoor unit. Each additional indoor unit is $100. This covers standard wall-mounted units; ducted, cassette and commercial systems are quoted after we see them.

How often should a mini-split be cleaned?

It depends on how much the system runs, pets, dust and cooking in the home. If you notice weaker airflow, odors, dark buildup on the vanes or water leaking from the indoor unit, it is time for a cleaning.

Is there a warranty on repairs?

Replacement parts are covered by the manufacturer or distributor warranty. We explain the coverage for your parts when we quote the repair.

Do you service commercial systems?

Yes. We service light commercial HVAC, including rooftop units and ventilation systems for shops, salons, gyms and offices.
