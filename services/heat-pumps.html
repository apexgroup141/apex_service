Why Heat Pumps Work Well in Puget Sound

One Heat Pump for Heating and Cooling Your Home

A heat pump is an HVAC system that can heat your home in winter and cool it in summer. During the summer, it works much like a central air conditioner, moving heat from inside the home to the outdoors. In winter, the process reverses: the system collects available heat from the outdoor air and transfers it into the home.

The key difference between a heat pump and electric resistance heating is that a heat pump moves heat rather than creating it directly. This allows heat pumps to provide efficient home heating while also serving as the home's central cooling system during the summer.

Heat pumps are especially well suited to the Puget Sound climate. Our winters are relatively mild compared with many colder parts of the country, and modern heat pumps are designed to operate at temperatures below those we typically experience here. A properly sized heat pump can meet a home's heating needs under regional design conditions while using the same system for cooling during summer.

A fully electric system can pair a heat pump with an air handler. If a homeowner wants to keep an existing natural gas or propane furnace, a dual-fuel configuration may also be possible. In many homes with central A/C and ductwork, upgrading to a heat pump may be possible without rebuilding the entire air-distribution system, provided the ductwork has appropriate size, condition and airflow.

Common questions

Heat Pump Installation FAQ

How much does heat pump installation cost?

Typical installed ranges are $7,500–$10,000 for Essential, $9,000–$13,000 for Comfort and $11,000–$16,000 for Premium options. Final pricing depends on home size, equipment, electrical requirements, ductwork and installation conditions.

Can a heat pump replace my furnace and AC?

In many homes, yes. A heat pump can provide heating and cooling, but the right configuration depends on the home's load, electrical capacity, ductwork and existing equipment.

Do heat pumps work in Washington winters?

Modern heat pumps can be well suited to Puget Sound conditions when the equipment is selected and sized around the home and regional design temperatures.

How long does installation typically take?

Installation timing depends on equipment configuration, electrical work, duct transitions, access, permits and other site conditions. The expected schedule is reviewed with the written project scope.

Is financing available?

Yes. Flexible financing options are available for qualifying projects. Final approval, rates and terms depend on the financing provider.

What labor warranty does Apex provide?

Apex provides a 10-year labor warranty on qualifying heat pump installations. Installed equipment is also covered by the applicable manufacturer warranty.
