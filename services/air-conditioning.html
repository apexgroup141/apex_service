Repair or replace

Should You Repair or Replace Your AC?

If your existing AC has a minor issue and the rest of the system is in good condition, repair may still make sense. If the system is older, unreliable, or facing a major refrigerant or compressor repair, replacement may be the better long-term choice.

Common questions

Air Conditioning FAQ

How much does central AC installation cost?

Pricing depends on system size, indoor coil requirements, refrigerant piping, electrical work, and installation complexity. We provide a written estimate after an in-home assessment.

Should I install AC or a heat pump?

If your furnace is still in good condition and you only need cooling, central AC may be a practical option. If you also need new heating equipment, a heat pump may make more sense.

Can I add AC to my existing furnace?

In many homes, yes. The existing furnace, blower, ductwork, electrical system, and available coil space need to be checked first.

Do I need new ductwork?

Not always. Many homes can use the existing duct system, although some projects benefit from airflow or transition improvements.

How long does AC installation take?

Many straightforward installations can be completed in one day, while more complex projects may take longer.

Can I finance AC installation?

Yes. Financing options are available for qualifying homeowners.

Do you repair existing AC systems?

Yes. Apex also provides diagnostic and repair service for existing air conditioning systems.

Do you provide free installation estimates?

Yes. We provide free in-home estimates for installation and replacement projects.
