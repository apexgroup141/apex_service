Project overview

Commercial rooftop service focused on equipment operation.

This commercial HVAC service project involved rooftop inspection and diagnostics for YORK rooftop units at a retail center in Tacoma, WA. The work included equipment inspection, control and economizer review, open-panel diagnostics, refrigerant performance checks, and service verification to help confirm proper rooftop unit operation.

Work performed

Inspection, controls review and diagnostics

01

Rooftop Inspection

  • Inspected rooftop unit condition and overall setup.
  • Reviewed equipment access and general operating condition.
  • Verified the rooftop installation environment.
02

Controls & Economizer Review

  • Opened service panels for inspection.
  • Checked control components and wiring.
  • Inspected the economizer and fresh-air control assembly.
  • Reviewed visible control condition and operation.
03

Diagnostics & Performance Check

  • Performed rooftop diagnostics.
  • Connected digital gauges for performance verification.
  • Checked operating readings and refrigerant-side performance.
  • Verified unit operation during service.
04

Service Verification

  • Confirmed rooftop equipment operation.
  • Completed inspection and service review for the commercial site.
  • Documented the visible condition and testing process.
