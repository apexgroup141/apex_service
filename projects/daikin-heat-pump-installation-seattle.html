Project overview

Modern Heating and Cooling for an Older Seattle Home

A homeowner in Seattle contacted Apex Service Group to improve heating in a small, older home. The house relied on aging electric resistance heaters that were no longer providing adequate comfort.

Our team installed a Daikin two-zone ductless heat pump system, giving the homeowner both heating and cooling in two areas of the home without adding traditional ductwork.

The challenge

Comfort, Placement, and Clean Routing

The existing electric heaters were struggling to keep the home comfortable. The homeowner wanted more dependable heating and the added benefit of air conditioning during warmer weather.

Because this was a compact, older house, indoor-unit placement, outdoor equipment positioning, and neat refrigerant line routing were important parts of the installation plan.

Our solution

A Daikin Two-Zone Ductless Heat Pump

We installed a Daikin multi-zone heat pump with two wall-mounted indoor units connected to one outdoor unit.

During startup, we were particularly impressed by how quietly both the indoor units and outdoor equipment operated—a welcome benefit for a smaller home.

Work completed

Installation Scope

  • Installed and securely mounted two Daikin wall-mounted indoor units.
  • Positioned the outdoor heat pump on an equipment pad with metal mounting supports.
  • Added an outdoor-unit stabilizing support to the wall.
  • Installed refrigerant lines and neat protective line-set covers along the exterior.
  • Completed the planned electrical connections and outdoor disconnect installation.
  • Performed refrigerant circuit leak testing and evacuation.
  • Commissioned the equipment and checked system operation.

Professional installation & commissioning

Careful Installation and Final Testing

Our team paid close attention to equipment placement, secure mounting, neat exterior routing, and refrigeration best practices.

Before startup, we checked the refrigerant circuit and performed a deep vacuum to remove air and moisture. We then completed startup procedures and checked system operation in its heating and cooling functions.

The result

Quiet, Independent Comfort in Two Areas

The homeowner now has a Daikin two-zone heat pump system that provides heating and cooling, with independent temperature control in the two areas it serves.

The quiet operation of both the indoor units and outdoor heat pump was especially noticeable during testing. The system was installed, started, and checked, and the homeowner was very pleased with the result.

This Seattle project shows how a ductless heat pump can bring modern comfort to an older home that previously relied on electric resistance heating.
