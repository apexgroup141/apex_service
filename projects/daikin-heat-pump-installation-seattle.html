Project Overview

A two-zone comfort upgrade for an older Seattle home.

A homeowner in Seattle contacted Apex Service Group to upgrade the heating system in a small, older home. The house relied on aging electric resistance heaters that were no longer providing adequate comfort.

Our team installed a modern Daikin two-zone ductless heat pump system, providing efficient heating and cooling for two separate areas of the home.

The new system offers independent temperature control, exceptionally quiet operation, and year-round comfort without the need to install traditional ductwork.

The Challenge

Improving comfort without extensive structural changes.

The home's existing electric heaters were struggling to maintain comfortable indoor temperatures. The homeowner wanted a more effective and reliable heating solution that would also provide air conditioning during warmer months.

Because the house was relatively small and older, equipment placement and installation quality were especially important. We needed to find suitable locations for both indoor units and the outdoor heat pump while keeping the exterior refrigerant lines neatly organized.

Our goal was to improve the home's comfort without extensive modifications to the existing structure.

Our Solution

Independent heating and cooling for two areas.

We installed a Daikin multi-zone ductless heat pump system featuring two wall-mounted indoor units connected to a single outdoor unit.

The system provides heating and cooling in two separate areas, allowing the homeowner to adjust temperatures independently according to the needs of each space.

One of the most impressive features we noticed during commissioning was how quietly the equipment operated. Both indoor units produced very little noticeable noise, and the outdoor heat pump was also remarkably quiet.

This is particularly valuable in smaller homes, where minimizing equipment noise can make a meaningful difference in everyday comfort.

Work Completed

Installation scope

  • Installation and secure mounting of two Daikin wall-mounted indoor units.
  • Placement of the outdoor heat pump on a dedicated equipment pad with metal mounting supports.
  • Additional stabilization of the outdoor unit using a wall-mounted support.
  • Installation of refrigerant lines and protective decorative line-set covers along the exterior walls.
  • Electrical connections and installation of an outdoor disconnect.
  • Refrigerant system leak testing and deep vacuum evacuation.
  • System startup, commissioning, and operational checks.

Professional Installation & System Testing

Careful mounting, line routing, and commissioning.

At Apex Service Group, we believe that proper installation is just as important as the equipment itself.

Throughout this project, our team paid close attention to secure equipment mounting, careful refrigerant line routing, and a clean overall appearance.

The outdoor unit was installed on a stable equipment pad with additional support, while the refrigerant lines were protected by decorative covers designed to maintain a neat appearance along the home's exterior.

Before starting the system, we performed refrigerant circuit leak testing and deep vacuum evacuation to remove air and moisture from the lines.

After completing the installation, we performed startup procedures and checked the system's operation.

These steps help ensure the equipment is properly prepared for reliable operation.

The Result

Quiet, independently controlled comfort in every season.

The homeowner now enjoys a modern Daikin two-zone heat pump system that provides comfortable heating during colder weather and efficient cooling during warmer months.

Each indoor unit can be controlled independently, allowing comfortable temperatures in different areas of the home.

Another particularly noticeable advantage was the exceptionally quiet operation of the system. Both the indoor units and outdoor heat pump operated with minimal noticeable noise during our testing.

After installation and commissioning, the homeowner was very pleased with the system's operation and the overall result.
