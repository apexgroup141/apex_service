Mini-Split Deep Cleaning & Maintenance in Seattle, WA

This Seattle home had Fujitsu ductless mini-split indoor units with internal dust and buildup that required more than a basic filter cleaning. Project overview

A deeper level of maintenance for a ductless system.

The indoor units needed service beyond routine filter care. Visible buildup was present on the blower wheels, filters, accessible plastic surfaces, and heat-exchange areas. Our team opened the units carefully and used a dedicated cleaning bag to contain water and debris while protecting the surrounding room. Service process

From visible buildup to cleaned and reassembled equipment

01

Condition Before Service

  • Inspected the Fujitsu indoor units and accessible components.
  • Documented dust and buildup on the blower wheels, filters, internal plastic surfaces, and heat-exchange areas.
  • Confirmed that a deeper cleaning was appropriate.
02

Protected Cleaning Setup

  • Carefully opened the indoor-unit housings.
  • Installed a dedicated mini-split cleaning bag around each unit.
  • Contained wash water and debris to protect walls, furniture, and floors.
03

Indoor Deep Cleaning

  • Cleaned the blower wheel and evaporator coil / heat exchanger.
  • Cleaned filters, housing, and accessible internal plastic components.
  • Addressed the accessible drain area before reassembly.
04

Outdoor Unit & Final Check

  • Inspected and cleaned the Fujitsu outdoor unit.
  • Reassembled the indoor equipment after cleaning.
  • Checked the system for normal operation and airflow.

Why periodic mini-split deep cleaning matters

Routine filter care is important, but dust can also accumulate deeper inside a ductless indoor unit. Periodic professional cleaning can help:

  • Maintain airflow through the indoor unit.
  • Reduce internal buildup and related odors.
  • Improve the cleanliness of accessible indoor-unit components.
  • Support efficient system operation.
  • Reduce unnecessary strain associated with restricted airflow.
