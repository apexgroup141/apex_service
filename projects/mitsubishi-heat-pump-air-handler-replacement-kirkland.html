Project overview

A gas furnace replacement planned around the existing home.

This Kirkland residential HVAC project replaced the existing gas furnace with a Mitsubishi Electric heat pump and air handler system. The new indoor equipment was connected to the home’s existing ductwork with fitted transitions, while the outdoor heat pump, condensate drainage, wall controller, and system connections were completed as part of the replacement. Final startup and testing confirmed the system was ready for operation.
