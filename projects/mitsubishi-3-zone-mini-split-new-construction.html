3-Zone Mitsubishi Mini-Split Installation – New Construction

Complete 3-zone Mitsubishi mini-split installation for a new-construction home in Seattle, Washington, including refrigerant line-set and condensate rough-in during framing, followed by installation of three wall-mounted indoor units and the outdoor heat pump system.

Location
Seattle, WA
System
3-zone Mitsubishi mini-split
Project stages
Rough-in and final installation

Project overview

A complete installation planned around construction.

This new-construction project was completed in two stages. During framing, our team installed and routed refrigerant line sets and condensate drains for three Mitsubishi mini-split zones before the walls were closed. The lines were carefully routed through the structure and brought together at a dedicated exterior location. After drywall and interior finishing were completed, we returned to install three Mitsubishi wall-mounted indoor units and the outdoor heat pump system, using the infrastructure prepared during the rough-in stage.

Two-stage process

From rough-in to final installation

Stage 1

Rough-in during construction

  • Installed line sets for three separate indoor units before drywall.
  • Routed refrigerant lines through wall cavities and ceiling or attic framing.
  • Secured the line sets neatly to the framing.
  • Installed condensate drain piping and prepared indoor-unit locations.
  • Brought all three line sets to a protected common exterior exit point.
Stage 2

Final equipment installation

  • Installed three Mitsubishi wall-mounted indoor units.
  • Connected the prepared refrigerant and condensate lines.
  • Installed the Mitsubishi outdoor heat pump at the prepared location.
  • Completed the refrigerant connections between the outdoor unit and all three zones.
